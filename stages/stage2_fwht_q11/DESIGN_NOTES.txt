Stage-2A design notes
=====================

Architecture:
  host/load port -> fwht_q11_stage2 adapter -> unchanged fwht_inplace core
  core internal memory: 4096 x 32-bit signed S32.F11, inferred as BRAM
  readback raw H coefficient
  normalization: signed arithmetic shift >>> 6
  readback normalized W coefficient remains 32-bit S32.F11

Why keep 32-bit after normalization:
  Stage-0.2 defines normalized coefficients, measurements, and residuals as
  signed 32-bit with 11 fractional bits. Some normalized coefficients exceed
  signed 16-bit Q5.11 range (cat64 maximum is 61415), so they must not be
  narrowed to 16 bits.

Core reuse:
  The existing Stage-4 FWHT source is copied unchanged. The wrapper only
  exposes the raw and normalized readback values.
  Stage-2B will use the same core for forward transform and adjoint transform
  sequentially; it will not instantiate two FWHT engines.

Mask/data convention:
  1024 measured coefficients are stored in ascending row-major flattened
  index order. mask_4096_rowmajor.mem and measurement_indices_1024.txt
  are checked against Stage-0.2 golden_vectors.npz.
